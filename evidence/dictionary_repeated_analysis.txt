Honggfuzz Dictionary Experiment - Repeated Comparison
======================================================

Target: libmobi v0.10
Sanitizer: AddressSanitizer
Initial seeds: 9 per experiment
Runtime: 3601 seconds per experiment
Threads: 1
Dictionary: 15 MOBI tokens

Crashes: 0

Crashes: 0

AVERAGE RESULTS

Without dictionary:
Iterations: 83136
New units: 1346.5

With dictionary:
Iterations: 78508.5
New units: 1358
Reported coverage: 19.5%

INTERPRETATION

Dictionary-enabled runs averaged 5.57% fewer iterations,
but 0.85% more new units.

There was no consistent coverage improvement and no
new crash was detected in these four experiments.

The results do not establish that dictionary-based
fuzzing is better or worse for this target.

LIMITATIONS

Only two runs per configuration were performed.
Execution speed varied between runs.
Reported coverage percentages are rounded.
Random mutation and system variability affect results.
A larger number of independent runs would be required
for a statistically reliable comparison.

CONCLUSION

The 15-token MOBI dictionary did not demonstrate
a clear, reproducible advantage in this experiment.
